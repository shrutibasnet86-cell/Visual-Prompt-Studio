**Tittle**  Visual Prompt Studio Phase 1.1
Prompt:  Create a large-scale digital projection artwork displayed on the side of a city hall building in a busy downtown square, designed as a social protest against gender inequality. The artwork serves to inspire public discussion and challenge societal norms. Its meaning is to highlight the ongoing struggle for equal rights and opportunities. The subject matter includes silhouettes of women breaking through glass barriers and standing in positions of leadership. The style is representational art, using bold imagery and strong contrasts to clearly communicate the message of empowerment and social change.

Vocabularly Breakdown Materials:

Digital sketches and images
High‑resolution digital artwork files

Tools:

Illustration/animation software (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects)
Projection‑mapping software (MadMapper, Resolume)
High‑brightness projectors and mounts

Processes:

Create concept and visuals
Design and animate artwork
Map visuals onto the building
Set up projectors and run the projection
